## Management, Control Procedures and Reports

### Project Control Functions

With every technology project, the challenge is to balance competing demands among scope, time, cost, quality, and the different stakeholders. GLOBALBIT Global Method for Project Management provides consistent processes for planning deliverables, managing change, assuring quality, and handling training activities and to maximize consistency and reusability. A few of the key project control processes include the following:

### Planning

Planning begins with fully understanding the customer's objectives and requirements. Once the requirements are understood, the next step is to establish a Work Breakdown Structure, the specific tasks required to accomplish the project objectives. Time estimates are made for each task using various estimating methods. Dependencies are determined and a Gantt Chart is created. A detailed Project Testing Plan is written and these two documents become the baseline by which the project is managed, tracked, and measured.

### Communication

Communication is effected through day-to-day contact between GLOBALBIT team members and CUSTOMER staff, through weekly status meetings and written reports. A determination is made as to what is important to the key stakeholders and the status reports are geared to satisfying their information needs. The reports will focus on schedule and key action items.

Globalbit assigns an experienced project manager early in the process to expedite agreement on the project objectives as well as specific tasks, plans, and costs. Customer's assigned project manager is a specialist in managing the latest information technology and tools as well as applying the Project Management methodology.

Globalbit has an extensive project management development program designed to make sure Globalbit project managers are continually educated on the technical skills of project management and leverage good practices both with Globalbit and within the industry.

Globalbit's highly skilled professional Project Managers, make the difference in utilizing a powerful, proven methodology in successfully managing the delivery of Customer's business solution.

## QA Methodologies

Quality is ensured using a systematic multi-stage approach:

- **Continuous Testing** — Developers run unit and integration tests as part of daily work
- **Sprint Testing** — Dedicated QA cycle at the end of each sprint
- **Regression Testing** — Full regression before each release
- **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** — Client-side validation before production deployment
- **Performance Testing** — Load and stress testing for production readiness

### Risk Assessment Template

| Risk | Severity (1-5) | Probability (1-5) | Risk Level | Hedging |
|------|:-:|:-:|:-:|---------|
| {{RISK_1}} | {{SEV}} | {{PROB}} | {{LEVEL}} | {{MITIGATION}} |

**Severity Scale:**

1. Negligible — No significant effect on project
2. Minor — Small delays or minor rework required
3. Moderate — Noticeable impact on timeline or quality
4. Major — Significant delays, budget overrun, or quality degradation
5. Critical — Project failure or unrecoverable damage

**Probability Scale:**

1. Very Low — Unlikely to occur (< 10%)
2. Low — Small chance (10–25%)
3. Medium — Possible (25–50%)
4. High — Likely (50–75%)
5. Very High — Almost certain (> 75%)

**Risk Level = Severity × Probability**

- 1–6: Low Risk (Monitor)
- 7–14: Medium Risk (Active mitigation required)
- 15–25: High Risk (Immediate action required)

## Performance Commitments & SLA

> **When to include**: Any enterprise client proposal above NIS 200K, or whenever the client asks about SLA, performance guarantees, or staff replacement. Transforms the proposal from "development services" to "partner with skin in the game".

Globalbit is committed to project success, not merely to delivering hours. Accordingly, the following commitments form an integral part of this proposal:

- **Production bug response times (post go-live):**
  - P0 (system down / data loss): Response begins within 1 hour, 24/7.
  - P1 (major functionality impaired): Response begins within 4 business hours.
  - P2 (non-critical issue): Response begins within 2 business days.
  - P3 (minor issue / enhancement request): Addressed at start of the next sprint.

- **Team Continuity:** Core team members are named in the Team section. Globalbit will not replace a core team member during the project without prior client approval. In case of departure or extended absence, Globalbit will provide an equal or higher caliber replacement within 5 business days at no additional cost.

- **Full Source Code Ownership:** Upon payment for hours actually delivered, full IP rights to the source code transfer to the client. The client has live repository access throughout the project - no "code held hostage".

- **Exit Plan:** Should the client elect to transition maintenance to an alternate vendor or internal team, Globalbit commits to a structured 4-week knowledge transfer from notice - including architecture documentation, operations runbook, build/deploy guides, and live sessions. Up to 40 hours of transfer time are included in the project cost at no additional charge.

- **Professional Liability Insurance:** Globalbit carries a NIS 5,000,000 professional liability policy. Certificate of Insurance available on request.

- **Transparent Billing:** Detailed monthly hours report (by task and team member), open to inspection. Unused hours are credited or rolled to the next month.
