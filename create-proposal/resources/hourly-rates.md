## Payment Terms Template

### Time & Materials (Default)
>
> The client will purchase a package of hours in advance based on the estimated monthly hours and the hourly rates specified in this proposal. Upon project completion, if there are any unused hours, the company will credit the client for the cost of the unused hours.
>
> Payment will be made on a Net + 5 basis.

### Fixed Price
>
> Payment will be structured in milestones tied to deliverables:
>
> - {{MILESTONE_1}}: {{PERCENTAGE}}% upon {{DELIVERABLE}}
> - {{MILESTONE_2}}: {{PERCENTAGE}}% upon {{DELIVERABLE}}
> - Final: {{PERCENTAGE}}% upon project completion and acceptance

### Retainer (Hours Bank)
>
> The client will purchase a monthly hours bank of {{HOURS}} hours at the blended rate of {{RATE}} ₪/hour.
> Unused hours will roll over for up to {{MONTHS}} months.
> Hours exceeding the monthly bank will be billed at standard rates.
