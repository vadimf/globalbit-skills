# Service Playbook: Maintenance & Support

> **When to use**: Client needs ongoing maintenance, support, bug fixing, feature development, or retainer-based services for an existing system.
> **Reference proposals**: CarWiz (Proposal 2)

---

## Overview

Maintenance & Support is a **recurring revenue** engagement. Position it as **protection** for the client's technology investment — not just reactive bug fixing but proactive health management.

### Key Selling Points

- Proactive monitoring prevents issues before they affect users
- Dedicated team = consistency and deep system knowledge
- Flexible model: scale hours up/down based on business needs
- Feature development pipeline keeps the product evolving
- Single vendor for both maintenance and enhancements reduces friction

---

## Service Model Components

### 1. Preventive Maintenance (30–40% of hours)

- System health monitoring and alerting
- Dependency updates (frameworks, libraries, OS patches)
- Security patches and vulnerability remediation
- Database optimization and cleanup
- Performance monitoring and tuning
- SSL certificate renewals, DNS management
- Backup verification and disaster recovery testing

### 2. Break-Fix Support (20–30% of hours)

- Bug triage and resolution
- Production incident response
- Root cause analysis
- Hotfix deployment
- Client-facing issue tracking and communication

### 3. Feature Development (30–40% of hours)

- New feature planning and estimation
- Design and implementation
- Testing and deployment
- Sprint-based delivery for larger features
- A/B testing support

### 4. Project Management & Communication (10–15% of hours)

- Weekly status reports
- Monthly review meetings
- Quarterly strategic planning sessions
- Backlog grooming and prioritization
- Stakeholder alignment

## SLA Tiers

| Level | Response Time | Resolution Target | Examples |
|-------|:-:|:-:|---------|
| **Critical (P1)** | 2 hours | 8 hours | System down, data loss, security breach |
| **High (P2)** | 4 hours | 24 hours | Major feature broken, payment processing issue |
| **Medium (P3)** | 8 hours | 48 hours | Non-critical feature malfunction, UI bug |
| **Low (P4)** | 24 hours | 5 business days | Enhancement request, minor cosmetic issue |

> **Note**: SLAs apply during business hours (Sunday–Thursday, 9:00–18:00 IST) unless extended support is agreed.

## Risks Specific to This Service

| Risk | Severity | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|:-:|:-:|-----------|
| Legacy code complexity limiting development speed | 3 | 4 | Initial code audit; gradual refactoring alongside feature work |
| Single point of failure on key team member | 3 | 3 | Knowledge sharing; documented procedures; cross-training |
| Scope creep — maintenance blurring into large projects | 3 | 4 | Clear boundary: features > {{X}} hours go through separate estimation |
| Underestimation of monthly hours needed | 3 | 3 | First month as assessment; adjust package after 30 days |
| Client dissatisfaction with pace of changes | 3 | 3 | Weekly demos; transparent hour tracking; priority alignment |

## Value Framing

- **Asset protection**: "Your system is a business-critical asset. Proactive maintenance protects your investment and prevents costly downtime."
- **Cost comparison**: "Dedicated maintenance at {{X}} hours/month costs a fraction of replacing a senior developer when issues arise."
- **Continuity**: "A team that knows your system deeply resolves issues 3x faster than a new team brought in for emergencies."
- **Evolution**: "Your product doesn't stand still. Continuous enhancement keeps you ahead of competitors and aligned with user expectations."
