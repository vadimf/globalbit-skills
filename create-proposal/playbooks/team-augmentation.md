# Service Playbook: Team Augmentation & Staff Extension

> **When to use**: Client needs to extend their development team with Globalbit engineers, either embedded in the client's team or working as a dedicated pod.
> **Note**: No specific example proposal — based on Globalbit's general offering and industry best practices.

---

## Overview

Team Augmentation is about providing **skilled professionals** who integrate into the client's existing processes and culture. Position it as **talent on demand** — faster than hiring, more flexible than fixed contracts, with Globalbit's quality standards built in.

### Key Selling Points

- Ramp-up in days, not months (vs. traditional hiring)
- Pre-vetted engineers with Globalbit's quality standards
- Flexible scaling: add or reduce team members based on project needs
- No recruitment costs, no HR overhead, no long-term commitments
- Globalbit's engineering culture: code reviews, best practices, continuous learning
- Optional PM overlay for coordination and quality assurance

---

## Engagement Models

### Model A: Embedded Engineers

- Globalbit engineers join the client's existing team
- Work within client's processes, tools, and ceremonies
- Client provides technical leadership and direction
- Globalbit provides the talent and quality assurance

### Model B: Dedicated Pod

- Self-managed team led by a Globalbit tech lead or PM
- Works on client-defined streams or modules
- Reports to client product owner
- Own ceremonies with sync points to client team

### Model C: Hybrid

- Core product managed by client's team
- Globalbit pod handles specific modules, features, or tech domains
- Shared ceremonies and tools

## Typical Proposal Structure Modifications

For team augmentation, modify the standard skeleton:

1. **Replace** "Proposed Solution" → "Team Composition" — detail the roles, experience levels, and responsibilities
2. **Replace** "Methodology" → "Engagement Model" — describe the integration approach
3. **Add** "Ramp-Up Plan" — how quickly engineers will be productive
4. **Add** "Communication & Reporting" — weekly syncs, daily standups, reporting cadence
5. **Replace** "Timeline" → "Engagement Duration" — minimum commitment, notice period for changes

## Team Augmentation Rate Card

Use standard rates from `resources/hourly-rates.md` with these adjustments:

- **Full-time dedication** (160+ hours/month): Standard rate applies
- **Part-time dedication** (80–160 hours/month): Standard rate + 10% flexibility premium
- **Minimum commitment**: Typically 3 months per resource
- **Notice period for changes**: 30 days for adding/removing team members

## Ramp-Up Plan Template

| Week | Activities |
|------|-----------|
| **Week 1** | Onboarding: environment setup, codebase walkthrough, tool access, meet the team |
| **Week 2** | Shadow work: pair programming, documentation review, initial small tasks |
| **Week 3** | Guided work: independent tasks with code review, increasing autonomy |
| **Week 4+** | Full productivity: independent contribution with standard review process |

## Quality Assurance Layer

Even in augmentation, Globalbit maintains quality standards:

- **Code reviews**: All code reviewed by Globalbit senior engineers (in addition to client reviews)
- **Best practices**: Engineers follow Globalbit's coding standards and security guidelines
- **Continuous learning**: Engineers participate in Globalbit's internal training programs
- **Replacement guarantee**: If a team member is not a good fit, Globalbit replaces them within 2 weeks

---

## Risks Specific to This Service

| Risk | Severity | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|:-:|:-:|-----------|
| Cultural mismatch between teams | 3 | 2 | Careful candidate selection; trial period; regular feedback loops |
| Knowledge silos forming around augmented engineers | 3 | 3 | Documentation requirements; pair programming rotation |
| Dependency on specific individuals | 3 | 3 | Cross-training; minimum 2 engineers for critical domains |
| Communication gaps with remote team | 2 | 3 | Overlapping working hours; daily standups; shared async tools |
| Scaling down leaving knowledge gaps | 3 | 2 | 30-day notice period; mandatory knowledge transfer sprint |
